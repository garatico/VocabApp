Sense disambiguator A short curated note on a word that shares its translation with another word, telling the two apart — Spanish "ser" and "estar" both mean "be", so "ser" carries the note "permanent" and "estar" carries "temporary". Table Quiz has independent switches for where it shows: next to the word, next to the meaning, and on the hover/click info card.
Grammar hint A short gender/number note automatically shown on a word whose own translation depends on the noun it modifies — an article or determiner like "a", "this", or "some". Lets you tell Spanish "un" from "una" apart before typing, without giving away the noun itself.
Gloss (or "sense") One accepted English translation of a word. A word can have several — English "bank" glosses to both "riverbank" and "financial institution" — and the Question/Answer glosses settings control how many show at once.
Gender indicator A visual marker — a small corner dot, a colored pill behind the word, or the whole cell tinted — showing whether a noun is grammatically masculine or feminine. Independent of the gender article below; a learner can use either, both, or neither.
Gender article The definite article prefixed to a noun once its gender is known, so it reads "la casa" instead of just "casa" — "the" has no gender in English, but most other languages this app teaches mark it on every noun.
Hint vs. Reveal Two separate, independently-switchable buttons next to an answer box. Hint uncovers one more letter of the answer with each click, up to one letter short of the full word. Reveal fills in the whole answer at once, and counts the word as missed.
Direction Which side of a word pair a Table quiz shows you and which side it asks you to produce. Word → Meaning shows the word and asks for its translation. Meaning → Word shows the translation and asks for the word. Mixed picks one of the two at random for each word.
Quiz Style How much of a Table quiz row stays visible before you answer it. Standard shows an editable answer box next to every word from the start. Recall hides the translation until you answer or reveal it. Double Recall hides both the word and the translation until then.
Regularity (Conjugation) How far a verb's conjugation strays from the standard pattern for its ending. Regular follows it exactly. Spelling-change needs a predictable spelling adjustment to keep its sound (Spanish "buscar" → "busqué"). Stem-change shifts a vowel in the stem ("pensar" → "pienso"). Irregular fits no pattern at all and has to be memorized.
CEFR level (Band) A1 through C2, the standard scale for language proficiency (the Common European Framework of Reference), used here to group words by how advanced they are rather than by raw frequency rank — the Level pool mode picks words this way instead of "Top N".
Domain A topic category a word belongs to — food, travel, business, and so on — used to narrow a quiz to vocabulary from a specific theme rather than by frequency or level.
Rank A word's position in frequency order for its language — rank 1 is the single most common word in that language. "Top 500" means ranks 1 through 500.
Smart List A saved search rule — "every B1 verb about food", say — rather than a fixed set of words picked by hand. A Smart List's contents update automatically as the underlying vocabulary or your progress changes.
Function word A grammatical particle with no real standalone translation — Japanese は ("topic marker") rather than an ordinary noun or verb. Marked apart from regular vocabulary since "translating" it word-for-word wouldn't mean anything on its own.
Script type / Romanized script For a language with a non-Latin writing system (Chinese, Japanese): the native script (Hanzi, Kanji, Hiragana, Katakana) versus its Latin-letter transliteration (Pinyin for Chinese, Romaji for Japanese) — typing the romanized form sidesteps needing an IME.
Testing Profile A saved combination of language, word count, and filter/quiz settings that can be reapplied later in one click, from that mode's own Testing Profiles button. Edited from My Lists' Testing Profiles section, which also has its own "Apply" button to push changes into the live quiz screen.
Mastered A word you've answered correctly enough times in Table or Picture Quiz to be marked as learned in My Lists. Tracked separately per language — mastering a word in Spanish has no effect on the same word's status in another language.
Simple Mode — what it changes The toggle for this lives in the toolbar to the right, always visible regardless of Advanced mode, and it's on by default. Turning it on: switches Advanced mode off and the swear-word filter on — both stay changeable afterward. Hides the My Content and My Lists tabs, the Testing Profiles button, the Part of Speech/Lists/Domains filter boxes, the "+ Languages" picker, the Skip Known size mode, and the Language indicator/Language appearance rows in Appearance — for as long as this stays on. The Part of Speech/Lists/Domains filters and "+ Languages" also stop applying whatever they were previously set to, not just disappear from view — so does Skip Known, which relies on the Lists filter. And it sets each mode to its simplest option: Table's Direction (Word→Meaning) and Quiz Style (Standard), Picture Quiz (Click the Picture), Conjugation's View (Grid) and Regularity (all), Trivia's Difficulty/Reading Difficulty (Easy) and Reading Length (Short), and Guess the Blank's Difficulty (Easy) — each still changeable by turning this off, same as Advanced mode and the swear filter.

Inline profile editing Adds "Full Profile" and "⚡ Quick Edit" buttons to each Testing Profile in the Profiles popover (Table/Picture/Conjugation), letting you tweak its parameters right there and try them out immediately — without saving over the profile unless you choose "Update" or "Save as new profile…".
Experimental quiz modes Shows the Picture Quiz, Trivia, Guess the Blank and Sentence Scramble tabs. These are proofs of concept — they work, but are unfinished, may change or be removed, and don't get the same polish as Table, Conjugation and My Lists.
Visual Profiles Shows a "Visual Profiles" sub-section under Testing Profiles in My Lists' sidebar — saved bundles of display preferences (Theme, Font Size) you can switch between, separate from what a Testing Profile quizzes on.
Confirm before removing a word edit My Content's "Edit an Existing Word" list asks before its Remove button resets every override on a word. Off skips the prompt.

On the web app, it only works when a real backend is reachable; otherwise the tab bounces you back here.
